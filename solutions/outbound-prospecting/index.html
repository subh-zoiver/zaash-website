Outbound Prospecting & Pipeline Building

Build the systematic prospecting engine you've never had. Generate consistent pipeline through targeted outbound—lists, sequences, tracking, and weekly reviews.

Why outbound isn't working for you

It's not that you haven't tried. It's that you've never built it as a system.

No consistent prospecting

Your team prospects when they remember or when the pipeline is empty. There's no daily/weekly cadence. Outbound happens in panic mode, not as a system.

Lists are random

Someone exports 500 contacts from ZoomInfo with no targeting criteria. Half aren't even in your ICP. You're spraying and praying.

No follow-up discipline

First email sent, no reply, and it dies. No sequence. No second touch. You're leaving 80% of potential conversations on the table.

Can't track what's working

You don't know which lists convert, which messages get replies, or which rep is effective. Flying completely blind.

What we build with you

A repeatable outbound motion that generates pipeline every week—not just when you panic.

Target Account Lists

Define your ICP, build segmented lists with clear targeting criteria, enrich with contact data. Know exactly who you're going after and why.

Multi-Touch Sequences

Email, LinkedIn, phone—designed as a coordinated campaign, not random touches. 5-7 touch sequences with A/B tested messaging.

Daily Prospecting Cadence

Set daily/weekly activity targets per rep. Build the muscle of consistent outbound. Track completion, not just results.

Weekly Pipeline Review

Review what's working—list performance, message performance, rep performance. Iterate based on data, not gut feel.

What you get

  • 3-5 segmented prospect lists (500-1000 contacts each)
  • Multi-touch sequences (email + LinkedIn + phone)
  • Prospecting playbook with templates
  • Activity tracking dashboard

How we work

01

ICP Workshop

We define your ideal customer profile—company size, industry, geography, tech stack, pain points. Build the targeting criteria that actually convert.

02

List Building

We build and enrich 3-5 segmented lists using your existing tools (Apollo, ZoomInfo) or our database. Each list has clear targeting rationale.

03

Sequence Design

We design multi-touch sequences tailored to each segment. Write messaging, set timing, define handoff to sales. A/B test and iterate.

04

Launch & Optimize

We launch sequences, train your team, run the first 4 weekly reviews with you, and provide 60 days of active optimization support.
