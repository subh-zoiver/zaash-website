Why your CRM is unreliable

Data is incomplete or wrong

Half your contacts have no email. Companies missing revenue data. Deals with no close date. Your team doesn't enter what matters.

Too many duplicates

Same contact 3 times, same company 5 times, all with different spellings. No one knows which record is real.

No one trusts the reports

Pipeline says ₹5Cr but you know it's actually ₹2Cr of real deals. Forecasts are fiction. Decisions made on gut feel instead.

No automation or workflows

Everything is manual. Tasks don't auto-create. Emails don't auto-send. Your ₹50L/year CRM is being used as a glorified Excel sheet.

What we build with you

A clean, automated CRM that your team actually uses and you can actually trust.

Data Cleanup & Deduplication

We merge duplicates, standardize fields, fill gaps with enrichment data, and archive dead records. One source of truth.

Field Configuration & Validation

Define required fields, set up picklists, create validation rules. Make it impossible to create junk records.

Automation & Workflows

Auto-create tasks, auto-send emails, auto-update fields based on triggers. Let the CRM do the admin work.

Reports & Dashboards

Build the 5-7 reports you actually need—pipeline health, conversion rates, rep performance, forecast accuracy. Real data, real decisions.
