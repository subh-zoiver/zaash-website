Turn events from relationship activities into pipeline activities

Most companies waste their event investment

You spend significant budget on events — booth, travel, sponsorship. You collect business cards. You come back with a list of names and good intentions. Three weeks later, nothing has moved. The Event GTM Tool changes that.

Pre-Event Targeting

Identifies which attendees match your ICP, prioritises them by fit and intent, and generates personalised outreach to schedule meetings before the event even starts.

During-Event Capture

Meeting scheduling, note capture, and action item extraction during the event. Every conversation logged and ready for follow-up before you leave the venue.

Post-Event Follow-Up

Automated follow-up sequences triggered immediately after the event — personalised to each conversation, sent at the right time, tracked to response.

ROI Measurement

Pipeline generated, meetings held, deals opened, and revenue attributed — all tracked back to each event so you know exactly what your event investment is worth.
