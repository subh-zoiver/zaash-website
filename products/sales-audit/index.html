Common questions

How is this different from a consulting engagement?

The audit is diagnostic only—we tell you what's broken and what to fix, but we don't fix it. If you want implementation, that's a separate engagement (Solutions). Many clients start with the audit to validate the problem before committing to a multi-month implementation.

Do you need access to our CRM?

For the full audit, yes. We need read-only access to analyze data quality, pipeline health, and conversion rates. For the free diagnostic, no access is required—it's based on your answers to 5 questions.

What if we don't have a CRM?

You still get value. We'll audit your process, tool usage, and capability gaps. The CRM section will highlight that as a critical gap and recommend next steps.

Can we get a discount if we commit to implementation after?

Yes. If you sign a Solutions engagement within 30 days of receiving the audit, the audit fee is credited toward the first invoice.

How long does the full audit take?

2 weeks from access granted to final presentation. Week 1: data extraction and analysis. Week 2: report preparation and presentation.

Is this specific to IT services companies?

The methodology works for any B2B company, but our benchmarks and recommendations are strongest for IT services, cybersecurity, and cloud/SaaS companies—that's where we have the most data.
