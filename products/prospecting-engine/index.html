Insight-Led Prospecting Engine

Not volume. Intelligence.

ICP-matched, intent-signalled, insight-driven outreach. Identifies the right accounts, generates the right message calibrated to the buyer's specific situation and industry context. The prospecting motion your team has never had.

Join the Waitlist

Volume prospecting is broken

Sending 500 emails a week to a scraped list is not prospecting. It is noise. Buyers have learned to ignore it. The Insight-Led Prospecting Engine does the opposite — fewer, better, more relevant outreach that actually gets replies.

Wrong accounts targeted

Most outreach goes to companies that look like your ICP on paper but are not actually ready to buy. Intent signals change everything.

Generic messages ignored

Copy-pasted templates with a first name swap. Buyers delete them before reading the second sentence. Personalisation at scale requires intelligence, not just mail merge.

No context, no relevance

Outreach that does not reference the buyer's specific situation — their industry, their recent news, their likely pain — is just noise with a name on it.

How the engine works

  • ICP Account Identification

    Identifies accounts that match your ideal customer profile — by firmographics, technographics, and buying signals — not just a list of companies in the right size range.

  • Intent Signal Detection

    Surfaces accounts that are showing buying intent right now — hiring for relevant roles, publishing relevant content, attending relevant events, or changing tools.

  • Insight-Driven Message Generation

    For each target account, generates an outreach message calibrated to their specific situation — their industry context, their likely pain, their recent activity — not a template.

  • Multi-Channel Sequencing

    Orchestrates outreach across email, LinkedIn, and phone in the right sequence and cadence for each account — based on what works for their profile.

  • Reply Intelligence

    Tracks every reply, categorises intent, and suggests the right next step — whether that is a meeting request, a follow-up, or a nurture sequence.
