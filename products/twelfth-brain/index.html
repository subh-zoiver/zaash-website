Twelfth Brain

Your AI-powered sales intelligence co-pilot

Twelfth Brain sits on top of your CRM and sales data, surfaces the insights your team needs before every call, and tells you exactly which deals to focus on this week.

Your CRM has the data. Nobody reads it.

The average B2B sales team has thousands of data points in their CRM — and uses almost none of it to make decisions. Twelfth Brain reads everything and surfaces only what matters, when it matters.

3 hours/week lost to CRM admin

Sales reps spend more time logging activities than selling. Twelfth Brain auto-captures and enriches data so your team can focus on conversations.

Wrong deals get attention

Without intelligence, reps chase the loudest deals, not the most likely ones. Twelfth Brain scores every deal and tells you where to spend your time.

Forecasts are guesswork

Pipeline reviews are based on gut feel and recency bias. Twelfth Brain builds forecasts from actual deal signals, not what your rep thinks will close.

What Twelfth Brain does

Deal Intelligence

Every deal gets a health score based on engagement, stage duration, stakeholder coverage, and historical patterns. Know which deals are real before your pipeline review.

  • Engagement scoring
  • Stall detection
  • Risk flags
  • Win probability

Pre-Call Briefs

Before every meeting, Twelfth Brain generates a 2-minute brief: what happened last time, what the buyer cares about, what to ask, and what objections to expect.

  • Meeting prep summaries
  • Stakeholder context
  • Suggested talk tracks
  • Objection alerts

Revenue Forecasting

AI-powered forecasts that go beyond stage-weighted pipeline. Twelfth Brain factors in deal velocity, rep history, and seasonal patterns to give you a number you can trust.

  • Commit vs. best case
  • Rep-level forecasts
  • Trend analysis
  • Scenario modelling

Activity Intelligence

Automatically capture emails, calls, and meetings. Enrich contacts with firmographic data. Flag accounts that have gone dark. Keep your CRM clean without manual effort.

  • Auto-capture activities
  • Contact enrichment
  • Engagement alerts
  • CRM hygiene scoring
