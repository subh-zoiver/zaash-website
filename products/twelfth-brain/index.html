Twelfth Brain

The CXO Intelligence Operating System

Every CXO accumulates extraordinary intelligence — from years of meetings, hundreds of conversations, thousands of decisions. Most of it is lost. Twelfth Brain is the permanent, growing, intelligent extension of their mind.

Your intelligence is leaking

Voice notes forgotten. Insights scattered across Slack, WhatsApp, email. Patterns that only exist in their heads, never captured, never compounded. The most valuable asset a CXO has — their accumulated intelligence — disappears the moment it is created.

Thoughts vanish instantly

Voice notes go unreviewed. Meeting insights are never written down. The best ideas surface in the shower and disappear before they are captured.

Knowledge stays siloed

Years of reading, conversations, and decisions exist in disconnected fragments — email, Notion, WhatsApp, memory. Nothing connects. Nothing compounds.

Influence requires effort

Publishing thought leadership takes hours of writing, editing, and scheduling. Most CXOs have the depth but not the bandwidth to make it visible.

The second brain that never forgets

Twelfth Brain captures everything they think, read, and say. It never forgets. It connects ideas over time, surfaces what they know at the moment they need it, and makes their accumulated intelligence available instantly — whether they are preparing for a meeting, making a decision, or publishing their thinking to the world.

Publishing is one output of that intelligence. LinkedIn posts, newsletters, podcast scripts — all grounded in the depth of their actual thinking, all in their authentic voice, all generated and ready with a single tap to approve. No ghostwriter. No content calendar. No performance. Just their intelligence, made visible.

What Twelfth Brain does

Universal Capture

  • Meeting capture
  • Web clipping
  • Cross-platform sync

Intelligence Connections

Twelfth Brain surfaces connections between ideas across months and years — linking a conversation from last quarter to a decision you are making today.

  • Pattern recognition

  • Timeline intelligence

Instant Recall

  • LinkedIn posts
